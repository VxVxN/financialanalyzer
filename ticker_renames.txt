# Securities whose MOEX secid changed. MOEX keeps the old trading history under
# the old secid, so without this a renamed company has no historical close for
# the years before the rename. The fetcher prices those years' market caps under
# the old secid instead, on that secid's own (frozen) ISSUESIZE — the share
# count at the time of the rename.
#
# Format:  SECID  OLD_SECID [OLDER_SECID...]   ("#" starts a comment)
#   SECID      current secid (the ticker the company is stored under)
#   OLD_SECID  former secids of the same security, newest first
#
# Only rename the same share (same ISIN). A different instrument (e.g. a GDR
# replaced by local shares) has a different share count and must not be listed.
