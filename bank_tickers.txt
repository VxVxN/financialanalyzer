# Bank ticker list for the fetcher — exchange-listed credit institutions whose
# quarterly figures come from the Central Bank's form 102 and 101 archives
# (https://www.cbr.ru/vfs/credit/forms/102-YYYYMMDD.rar, 101-YYYYMMDD.rar).
# Banks report to the CBR, not ГИР БО, so they are fetched through a separate
# pipeline.
#
# Format:  TICKER  REGN  CATEGORY   (one per line, "#" starts a comment)
#   TICKER   MOEX security id (also used for year-end market cap / P/E)
#   REGN     CBR registration number (the key into form 102, NOT the INN)
#
# Usage:   POST /api/fetch from /updates (empty lists refresh stored banks too)
#          banks=T on that request for a subset
#
# CAVEATS (inherent to the source — see internal/scraper/cbr docs):
#   * Form 102 is RSBU of the bank legal entity, not consolidated IFRS, so
#     figures differ from group/"market" numbers.
#   * NET PROFIT, REVENUE and EQUITY are populated. Revenue is a proxy: net
#     interest income + gross fee and commission income (form 102). Equity is
#     balance-sheet equity from form 101 (capital accounts 1xx + financial
#     result 706-708) at each quarter end; ROE and P/B are based on it.
#   * EBITDA/Debt have no meaningful bank equivalent and stay empty.
#   * Per-quarter net profit and revenue are recovered by differencing the
#     cumulative YTD archives; market cap, P/E and ROE are on the Q4
#     (full-year) row only.
